# Evidence and sources

Reviewed 26 October 2026. These are public primary sources. National statistics establish context; they do not prove Sanket's effectiveness or local prevalence.

1. MY Bharat, Hack for Social Cause / VBYLD 2027: https://mybharat.gov.in/pages/hack-for-social-cause . Timeline displays 27 October 2026 as the submission deadline; FAQ Q30 still says 15 October. Template requires 6-7 slides and AI-use disclosure. Public page also conflicts on AICTE/AISHE institution wording. Registered entrants should use their live portal requirements and official clarification where needed.
2. Ministry of Home Affairs / PIB, financial cyber-fraud figures: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2226441&lang=1&reg=1 . 2025: 24,02,579 financial-fraud complaints and INR 22,495 crore amount reported. Data is dynamic. This is reported amount, not an audited finding that every report represents confirmed loss or a unique victim.
3. PIB, cyber-safety workshop and response guidance: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2301973&lang=2&reg=48 . Supports prompt 1930 reporting, bank contact through official channels, credential caution and risks of remote-access misuse. Do not conflate its broader complaint figure with the financial-complaint series in source 2.
4. National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in/ . Official complaint destination. Sanket is not integrated with it.
5. Department of Telecommunications, Chakshu launch release (4 March 2024): https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/SancharSaathiDocuments/ImportantDocuments/DoT%20Press%20Release-%20Hon%27ble%20Union%20Minister%20of%20Communications%20Shri%20Ashwini%20Vaishnaw%20launches%20Digital%20Intelligence%20Platform%20%26%20Chakshu%20Module%20on%20Sanchar%20Saathi%20-%2004.03.2024.pdf . Distinguishes suspected fraudulent communications from victim reporting through 1930/cybercrime.gov.in. Service: https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/sfc/ .
6. UN SDG 16, target 16.3 (access to justice): https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal16 . Sanket's contribution is conceptual: easier navigation to official reporting, not a verified change in a UN indicator.
7. UN SDG 4, target 4.4 (relevant skills; ICT-skills indicator): https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal4 . Practice supports digital-safety learning; no learning improvement has yet been measured.

## Claims not made

No partnerships, user interviews, deployments with police, recovered funds, pilot results, detection accuracy, patent novelty or beneficiary counts are claimed. The product addresses a hypothesized preparation gap that the pilot must test. Existing official services remain the reporting authority.
